# Trance patterns learned (statistics only)

Measured on about 190 lead lines, 530 chord files and a large set of bass and sequence loops from a private 2000s trance MIDI collection (not in this repo, licences forbid redistribution). Only the numbers below were kept. The melodies in `tools/trance_melodies.py` are written by hand.

## Lead lines (minor keys)
- Scale degrees: root 34 %, 2nd 14 %, minor 3rd 15 %, 5th 13 %, minor 7th 10 %, 4th 8 %, minor 6th 6 %. Major 3rd, major 6th and 7th are rare.
- 38 % of moves repeat the same note, 22 % are steps of 1 or 2 semitones, 29 % are leaps of 5 or more, and octave jumps (up or down) are 13 %.
- Up and down moves are balanced (31 % / 30 %).
- Timing: 8th note positions are about twice as common as odd 16ths. Beat 1 is the most common onset (11 %). 69 % of notes are one 16th long, 23 % two, only about 5 % three or longer.
- Typical range: 14 semitones (middle half 10 to 19).

## Chords (per bar, minor keys)
- i is 29 % of all bars, then VI 16 %, iv 10 %, v 10 %, III 10 %, VII 10 %. A major V is rare.
- Most common moves: i to VI, VI to i, i to VII, iv to i, i to v, i to iv, i to III.
- Most common loops: two chord vamps that alternate with the tonic (i VI, i iv, i III, i VII), then i VII VI i, i VII v VI, i VI III VII.
- 55 % of files start on i.

## What this changed
- Added pulsing, repeated-note melodies with octave jumps and a moving tonic pedal (Pulse motif, Pedal pump, Stepwise hook, Sunrise hook, Falling vamp).
- Kept the slow anthem pieces for breakdown style.
