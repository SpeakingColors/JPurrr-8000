JPurrr-8000

A free and open source synth in the spirit of the late 90s JP series, with a mix ready FX chain. Works 100% offline.

This installer puts:

  • the VST3 plugin into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • the standalone app into /Applications
  • the manual and an uninstaller into /Library/Application Support/Studio 87/JPurrr-8000

Close your DAW first, then rescan plugins afterwards. Your presets and settings are never removed.
